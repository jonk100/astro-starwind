# re:MindMatters — Experience Principles

## Core Experience Goal

re:MindMatters aims to cultivate a mental well-being habit by bridging inspiring, positive editorial reading with practical daily application. 

The experience must feel:
- somatic (designed to lower physical and cognitive stress)
- empowering (validating current feelings and immediately unlocking personal agency)
- unhurried (encouraging slow, deliberate reading and deep, private writing)
- structured (anchoring thoughts in clear habits and spatial safety)

---

# First Impression (The 10-Second Principle)

Within the first 10 seconds of landing on the site or opening the app, a user must experience:
- **Immediate Somatic Relief**: The clean ivory background, spacious margins, and soft sage highlights signal that this is a safe, quiet space.
- **Cognitive Clarity**: No notification counts, no immediate requests to log in, no popups, and no flashing panels.
- **Atmospheric Warmth**: The typography and layout feel like entering a physical reading room or a beautifully crafted botanical garden.

---

# The Restorative Flow Architecture

To translate inspiring reading into active psychological growth, the platform guides the user through a frictionless, self-reinforcing loop:

```
[ READ ] ──> validating cognitive science / positive news
   │
   ├──> [ WRITE ] ──> private processing of prompt (Write App)
   │
   └──> [ ACT ] ──> daily behavioral tracking (Habit App)
```

1. **Step 1: Read (Cognitive Insight)**: The user reads a positive essay or self-care guide, understanding the structural context or science of their feelings.
2. **Step 2: Write (Emotional Expression)**: The user clicks the prompt hook at the end of the text, immediately opening the **Write/Journal app** to privately process their thoughts.
3. **Step 3: Act (Behavioral Reinforcement)**: The user adds a concrete micro-habit to their **Habit Tracker app** dashboard to build regular somatic or cognitive routines.

---

# Progressive Disclosure & Cognitive Load

We believe that too many choices breed analysis paralysis and anxiety. The interface follows strict progressive disclosure rules:

- **Frictionless focus**: While the user is writing in the Editor (`.tiptop-editor`), all adjacent UI elements—including sidebar navigation, top menus, and margin widgets—recede in visual weight or fade entirely.
- **The "Control" Gutter Trigger**: Keyboard shortcut panels and margin hints in the left/right gutters remain completely invisible by default. They smoothly slide into view **only** when the user presses and holds the `Control` key, and fade instantly upon release.
- **Opaque Context Menus**: Formatting menus, color pickers, and options popovers appear exactly at the point of interaction. Because they are 100% solid and opaque, they block out surrounding text, keeping the user focused on the active choice.

---

# Non-Linear Mood-Centric Navigation

Unlike traditional blogs that organize content chronologically (a system that breeds a sense of "catching up" and FOMO), re:MindMatters is navigated relationally:

- **Mood States**: Users discover content based on how they currently feel (e.g., *Overwhelmed*, *Scattered*, *Reflective*, *Seeking Growth*).
- **Growth Spheres**: Content is grouped into somatic, cognitive, and relational well-being areas.
- **Lateral Discovery**: Every article naturally maps to related prompts, complementary habits, or parallel positive developments in psychology.

---

# Spatial Grounding Principle

An interface that shifts, jumps, or alters its layout causes micro-anxiety. re:MindMatters enforces spatial grounding:
- The notepad slab sits centered on a stable vertical canvas.
- Gutter widgets stack in designated tabs on the right margin, maintaining a predictable vertical arrangement.
- Left-gutter and right-gutter layout columns maintain fixed, stable margins, giving the interface the reassuring structural gravity of a physical desk.
